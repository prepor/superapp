# CR-007 — threads: the row is the conversation, the panel is the whole of
# it.
#
# Assertions are the clicks: a closed message is addressed by its sender
# and the line it previews, an open one by its sender and date, so "this
# message opened in place" is checked by the run rather than by a human
# reading a screenshot.
wait 900

# The GitHub row folds six mails — five archived runs and the unread one.
# Opening it previews the thread: the five old runs are one line each, the
# unread one is open at the foot, and the list is scrolled to it.
click "CI failed"
wait 700
shot th1-github-thread

# A closed row opens in place when touched. The oldest run failed and
# carries the red status line, which is what its row previews.
click "GitHub: ci: FAILED"
wait 600
shot th2-old-run-opened

# The same row, open, is addressed by sender and date; touching it closes.
click "GitHub · aug 28 06:10"
wait 600
shot th3-old-run-closed

# Max's thread: my note (in Sent), his reply, his second reply with a
# quoted tail. The row reads "Max, me · 3"; the newest inbox mail opens,
# its quote folded behind one line — which unfolds when touched.
click "superapp panel model"
wait 700
shot th4-max-thread
click "quoted"
wait 500
shot th5-quote-unfolded

# Archiving files the conversation: both of Max's inbox mails go, the row
# goes with them, and the cursor carries on to the next thread. One cmd+z
# puts both mails and the row back.
key cmd+a
wait 700
shot th6-archived-thread
key cmd+z
wait 700
shot th7-undone
click "superapp panel model"
wait 600

# A mail passed on wears the forwarded mark by its date — the $Forwarded
# keyword, read from and pushed to the server like the read flag.
click "invoice 2026-08"
