# Telegram's first round: the surface over the demo world. A store nobody
# has booted comes up on mail's inbox, so the chat list is reached the way
# a person reaches it — through the launcher. Assertions are the clicks: a
# label that does not resolve fails the run, so "the chat opened on that
# group" is checked by the run rather than by a human reading a screenshot.

# A press on a row moves the cursor and previews the chat: it lands joined to

# opened on that group.

# jump to the end for the sticker and voice note. The first press took the

# the bar's `about` — and typing replace it, enter writes it, and the
