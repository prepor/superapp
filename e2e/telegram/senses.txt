# The senses, through the attach panel: the microphone and the camera as a
# person reaches them — `attach` on the chat's bar, then `voice`, `camera`
# or `video`. Headless has neither device, so what answers is the kernel's
# fake capture, which writes real files: a real Ogg Opus for a voice note, a
# real JPEG for a shot. That is the point of the fake — a suite sends what a
# person would.
#
# Nothing leaves a demo world, so a send is read the way `basic.txt` reads
# one: the strip goes and the list comes back empty.

wait 900
key cmd 2
wait 500
type "chats"
wait 500
key enter
wait 900
click "Vera Kovac"
wait 900

# -- a voice note ---------------------------------------------------------------

# `attach` opens joined to the chat and says nothing goes yet. `voice`
# starts the microphone: the strip says what and how long, the clock runs,
# and the meter under it draws what the capability is hearing.
click "attach"
wait 900
click "nothing goes with the next message yet"
wait 300
click "voice"
wait 2200
click "recording voice 0:02"
wait 200
shot s1-recording

# `send` stops it and sends it on its own — a toast in a demo world, and the
# Ogg Opus left under `captures/` for the engine that is not there. The
# strip goes and the list is back.
click "send"
wait 700
click "nothing goes with the next message yet"
wait 300

# -- the camera -----------------------------------------------------------------

# `camera` opens it with two verbs of its own. A shot lands on the chat's
# carried list and the camera stays up for the next one; `done` puts the
# list back with the shots on it, each row saying what it goes as.
#
# The fake numbers its files by the ones it has written, and the voice note
# above was the first, so these two are the second and the third.
click "camera"
wait 700
click "the camera is on"
wait 300
click "shoot"
wait 700
click "shoot"
wait 700
shot s2-camera
click "done"
wait 700
visible "capture-2.jpg · photo"
visible "capture-3.jpg · photo"
shot s3-carrying

# The chat counts them on its own bar, where `attach 2` is the link back —
# and the composer's CARRIES line names them. Two pictures leave together,
# as one album, wherever there is a wire to leave on.
key cmd+left
wait 500
click "attach 2"
wait 700

# -- a video message ------------------------------------------------------------

# `video` takes the camera and the microphone together, and the clock runs
# as it does for a voice note. There is no camera behind a headless run, so
# the picture's box stays empty and the strip is what says it is recording.
# `esc` throws it away, and the shots are still waiting underneath.
click "video"
wait 1500
click "recording video message 0:01"
wait 200
shot s4-video-message
key esc
wait 500
visible "capture-2.jpg · photo"
quit
