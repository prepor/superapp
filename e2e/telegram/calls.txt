# A call, from the person's card. This fixture has no account and therefore
# no worker, so nothing moves the call on: it stands at *contacting…* until a
# verb, which is what makes the walk deterministic under virtual time. The
# engine is the fake one, and it is never asked to carry anything — the wire
# never answers, so the call never reaches the state that would start it.
#
# The sounds are the same story: they are written under the store's own
# directory at first use, and a scripted run has none, so this walk is silent.
wait 900
key cmd 2
wait 500
type "chats"
wait 500
key enter
wait 900
click "Vera Kovac"
wait 700
click "about"
wait 700

# *voice call* rings and opens the panel on the ringing, joined to nothing.
click "voice call"
wait 900
click "contacting…"
wait 300
shot telegram-call-contacting

# Ending it is the one way out while it rings. With no wire to answer, the
# panel settles it itself and says how it ended.
click "end"
wait 700
click "call ended"
wait 300
shot telegram-call-ended

# And *close* is all that is left on the bar. The card has the focus again
# behind it, and its other call opens the same panel on the same person.
click "close"
wait 700
click "video call"
wait 900
click "contacting…"
wait 300
click "end"
wait 500
click "close"
wait 700
quit
