# A click inside a panel focuses it — through the shell's real mouse path
# (`mouse`), which is where a physical click's key-focus rule runs.
#
# The assertion is a keyboard one: `key down` walks the inbox only if the
# inbox holds focus, and the walk previews the next row, whose sender link
# then resolves in the previewed panel.
wait 900
mouse "help"
wait 500
shot focus-1-help

# A row click previews AND focuses the list it lives in.
mouse "Sat hike"
wait 700
shot focus-2-row-clicked
key down
wait 700
click "digest@rss.local"
wait 700
shot focus-3-walked-from-list

# The filter: the click lands in the field (typing goes there) and the
# panel takes focus with it.
mouse "help"
wait 500
mouse "filter"
wait 400
type "vera"
wait 500
shot focus-4-filter-typed
click "Q3 infra"
wait 700
quit
