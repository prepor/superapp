# The undo walk. Every action is one node of a tree: the layout before and
# after it, plus what it claimed of the world, so `cmd+z` puts both halves
# back — the panels where they stood and the rows as they were. `cmd+shift+z`
# goes forward again.
#
# A focus walk and a workspace switch are attention, not actions, and record
# nothing; a move between workspaces is an action, and one `cmd+z` teleports
# the panel and the viewport together.
#
# The clicks are the assertions: a label that only resolves in the state the
# undo restored is the proof that it was restored.

wait 900

# Opening a conversation is an action — it marks the mail read — and the
# reader lands joined to the right.
click "Q3 infra"
wait 900
shot u1-opened

# Archive: the conversation leaves the inbox and the reader closes behind it,
# and the list carries on to the next mail and previews it. That walk is the
# filing arriving at its consequence, not a second thing the person did, so
# all of it is one node.
click "archive"
wait 900
shot u2-archived

# One step back — the archive and the preview it moved on to together — and
# the reader is on the same letter again, which is what the click proves.
key cmd+z
wait 900
shot u3-undone
click "Vera Kovac · aug 31 09:14"
wait 700

# Redo walks the one node forward: filed again, and the list moved on again.
key cmd+shift+z
wait 900
shot u4-redone

# Back once more, then close the list — which takes its joined reader with it
# — and take that back too.
key cmd+z
wait 900
key cmd+w
wait 900
key cmd+z
wait 900
click "Sat hike"
wait 900
shot u5-close-undone

# A move between workspaces is one node: undo brings the panel and the
# viewport back together.
key cmd+shift+3
wait 900
key cmd+z
wait 900
click "that airport book"
wait 900
shot u6-move-undone
quit
