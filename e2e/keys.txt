# CR-003 — the accelerators, driven by their chords alone.
#
# Assertions here are the clicks: every `click` names something that must
# exist by then, and a label that does not resolve is a failure. So a walk
# that lands on the wrong mail, or a reply that never opened, fails the run
# without needing a human to read a screenshot.
wait 900

# Open the newest mail (id 1) so the walk has somewhere to go.
click "Q3 infra"
wait 700
shot k1-message

# cmd+o walks older, in place (dotted semantics) — the binding that used to
# be a hidden `j`. Two steps lands on "Re: superapp panel model" (id 3).
key cmd+o
wait 600
key cmd+o
wait 600
shot k2-older-twice

# cmd+n walks back toward newer; one step is "[stelaxis] CI failed" (id 2).
key cmd+n
wait 600
shot k3-newer-once

# The panel really is that mail: its own from-link resolves.
click "GitHub"
wait 700
shot k4-contact
key cmd+w
wait 600

# cmd+r replies. The compose must exist — `discard` only resolves if it did.
key cmd+r
wait 700
shot k5-reply
click "discard"
wait 600
shot k6-discarded

# cmd+a archives the message panel and closes it. The inbox is what is left,
# so its own accelerator (cmd+r, refresh) is what must resolve next.
key cmd+a
wait 700
shot k7-archived

# Back on the inbox: cmd+r is refresh here, not reply — accelerators are
# panel-scoped. It toasts rather than navigating, so the inbox stays.
key cmd+r
wait 600
click "Sat hike"
wait 700
shot k8-inbox-alive
quit
