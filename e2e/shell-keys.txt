# The workspace's reserved chords, and the first rung of the routing order.
#
# Cmd is the workspace modifier. A chord is offered to the reserved set first
# — arrows and digits with and without shift, `w`, `z`, `u`, `t`, `i`, `l`, the
# column keys and `enter` — and only then to the focused widget, the focused
# panel's bar, and the bar of what it previews. A panel with four letters on
# its bar still loses `cmd+u` to the workspace, and that is the rule this
# suite walks.
#
# The bold letters and the last two rungs are `shell-accels`; the digits are
# `shell-workspaces`; the column keys are `shell-tabs`; `cmd+z` is `shell-undo`
# and `cmd+i` is `shell-context`.

wait 900

# -- `cmd+u`: the history, wherever the caret is ------------------------------

key cmd+u
wait 700
shot k1-history
key esc
wait 600

# -- the arrows walk focus ----------------------------------------------------

# A press on a row previews the conversation joined to the right and leaves
# focus on the list.
click "Q3 infra"
wait 900

# `cmd+right` moves focus onto the reader; `cmd+w` then closes *that* panel.
# Had focus stayed on the list, the list would have gone and taken its joined
# chain with it, and the row below would resolve nowhere.
key cmd+right
wait 700
key cmd+w
wait 900
click "Sat hike"
wait 900
shot k2-focus-walked

# -- shift walks the panel instead --------------------------------------------

# Moving the joined reader away un-joins it: it lands in a column of its own,
# still on its letter.
key cmd+right
wait 700
key cmd+shift+left
wait 900
click "Elena Petrova · aug 30 18:20"
wait 700
shot k3-panel-moved

# -- a reserved letter never reaches a bar ------------------------------------

# The reader has focus and wears four letters of its own, none of them the
# workspace's. `cmd+u` is the workspace's: the history goes up over the reader
# rather than the reader hearing a `u` at all. `esc` puts it away again and the
# reader is where it was, still on the same letter — nothing on that bar ever
# saw the chord. (The click above folded that letter shut, so the line it
# answers to now is the folded one.)
key cmd+u
wait 700
shot k4-history-over-a-bar
key esc
wait 700
click "Elena Petrova: Weather looks fine"
wait 700

# -- a plain key is typing, and belongs to the panel --------------------------

# `/` puts the caret in the list's filter; what follows narrows the list, which
# is what the row that still resolves proves.
key cmd+right
wait 700
key /
wait 600
type "vera"
wait 900
shot k5-filtered
click "Q3 infra"
wait 900
shot k6-opened-from-the-filter
quit
