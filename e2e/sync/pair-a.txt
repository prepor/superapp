# Device A: shows the ticket, writes a note, and waits for B's.
#
# Two processes, two stores, two endpoints on 127.0.0.1. Opening *device
# sync* is what makes a ticket at all; the scripted world drops it in the
# file `SUPERAPP_E2E_TICKET_OUT` names, and pair.sh starts B with it pasted
# into its own walk. Nothing here waits for that: A keeps the panel open and
# its note written while B catches up, and the last step is what proves the
# pairing carried something in this direction.

wait 900

# -- the ticket --------------------------------------------------------------

# Opening the panel opens the pairing window — sixteen fresh random bytes,
# and a ticket for as long as the panel is up. `pair with` resolving is the
# proof that the form really drew.
key cmd 2
wait 500
type "device sync"
wait 500
key enter
wait 1200
click "pair with"
wait 300

# -- something to carry ------------------------------------------------------

# A note, written before B is even up: its cells wait in this device's log,
# and the exchange that follows the pairing carries them.
key cmd 2
wait 500
type "notes"
wait 500
key enter
wait 900
click "new note"
wait 800
click "editor"
type "alpha from A"
wait 600
key cmd+w
wait 900
visible "alpha from A"

# -- and the other direction -------------------------------------------------

# The frames B needs to pair, read this note, and write one of its own. The
# two clocks are independent, so this wait is real time as much as virtual.
wait 48000
visible "beta from B"
quit
