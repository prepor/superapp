# The row is the conversation, the panel is the whole of it.
#
# Assertions are the clicks: a closed message is addressed by its sender and
# the line it previews, an open one by its sender and date — so "this message
# opened in place" is checked by the run rather than by a human reading a
# screenshot. The two readings are addressed apart, `mail body` and `mail
# html`, so a conversation holding one of each proves that neither leaves its
# words behind for the other.

wait 900

# Max's conversation folds three letters into one row: my note (in Sent), his
# reply, and his second reply with a quoted tail. Opening it previews the
# whole thread — the older two closed, one line each, the newest open. That
# newest letter came from a composer that writes HTML, so the reader draws it
# through the `Html` widget.
click "superapp panel model"
wait 900
shot h1-thread
click "mail html"
wait 500

# A closed row opens in place when pressed. It is addressed by its sender and
# the first line its author wrote, which is what it previews.
click "Andrey Rudenko: Wrote up the panel model"
wait 700
shot h2-opened

# That one is plain text, and both readings are now on screen at once — one
# letter drawn through the widget, one as a selectable run. A drag across the
# plain one is a real selection, which is what a passage one wants to quote
# back needs.
selectall "mail body"
wait 500
shot h3-both-readings

# The same row, open, is addressed by sender and date; pressing it folds the
# letter back and the panel asks for fewer rows again.
click "Andrey Rudenko · aug 29 14:02"
wait 700
shot h4-closed

# The newest letter came open with the panel, and the quote it was written
# over is folded behind one line. A composer writes that quote as a
# `<blockquote>` under an attribution line, and the fold reads it by the same
# rule it reads a `>` block — so one press unfolds it in place, whichever
# reading the letter arrived in.
click "› quoted"
wait 700
shot h5-quote-unfolded
click "mail html"
wait 500

# A link in that tail is a link of the letter: every run one drew is a
# rectangle of its own, so the pointer over it is a hand and not the
# reading's I-beam. Folded, there is no such rectangle — the letter Max
# wrote has no link in it — so this is the quote's.
drag "link" 30 0
wait 400

# The whole conversation files as one: both of Max's inbox letters go, the row
# goes with them, and undo brings all of it back.
click "archive"
wait 900
shot h6-archived
key cmd+z
wait 900
key cmd+z
wait 900
click "superapp panel model"
wait 800
shot h7-undone
quit
