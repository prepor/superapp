# args:
# env:
#
# A correspondent, found and read: the launcher's mail source answers a
# question with the people who wrote before the letters they wrote, the card
# says how much they have written, and the one link off it opens the inbox
# filtered to their address.
#
# Assertions are the clicks. "Vera Kovac" resolving in the launcher is proof
# the sender source answered; the card's address resolving is proof the card
# opened on it; and the filtered list's title is what proves the argument
# reached the panel rather than the field alone.

wait 900

# The launcher asks every app's source. Mail answers with the person first —
# a contact is a fact about the mailbox, not a document in it — and then with
# the letters, as the index ranks them.
key cmd 2
wait 400
type "kovac"
wait 700
shot c1-launcher
click "Vera Kovac"
wait 800
shot c2-card
click "vera@kovac.io"
wait 300

# The card's link: the inbox, filtered to the address. Its title carries the
# filter, which is what proves the panel's own argument arrived — the field
# is seeded from it and is the person's from then on.
click "messages from vera"
wait 900
shot c3-from
click "Q3 infra"
wait 700
shot c4-previewed

# The filter is a filter, not a wall: emptying the field brings the rest of
# the inbox back, and the letters that were hidden resolve again.
key /
wait 300
key cmd+a
key backspace
wait 500
key esc
wait 500
click "that airport book"
wait 700
shot c5-cleared

# A letter's own words reach it too — the index reads the body, which the
# headers alone never did.
key cmd 2
wait 400
type "thermos"
wait 700
shot c6-body-search
click "Sat hike"
wait 800
shot c7-found
quit
