# args:
# env:
#
# A letter to a group, and the header that says so.
#
# The `To` line of a real letter is a list — four people on it here, and a
# fifth in copy — and a single-line field can only show the first of them.
# So the reader's header names who the conversation is with, three of them
# and a count, and the count is a press: it unfolds every one of them, in
# full, with the copies marked.
#
# Assertions are the clicks. `4 people` resolving is proof the header counted
# what it folded; the addresses resolving under it is proof the fold opened.

wait 900

# Elena's Saturday walk: the one letter of the demo world addressed to more
# than one person.
click "Sat hike"
wait 900
shot p1-folded

# Three first names on the line, because several people do not fit under
# their full names in a panel this wide — and the account it arrived at is
# not among them: a person knows they are in their own mail.
click "mail people"
key cmd+a
copy "Max, Vera, Ana"

# The count says how many there are, not how many are hidden — press it and
# the whole list is under the line, one person a line, address and all. The
# copy wears the one mark that says it is one.
click "4 people"
wait 500
shot p2-unfolded
click "mail people list"
key cmd+a
copy "Max Ivanov <max@ivanov.dev>\nVera Kovac <vera@kovac.io>\nAna Marić <ana@maric.hr>\nTom Weber <tom@weber.de> · cc"

# The same control folds it back — it says `fewer` while the list is open,
# which is the only thing it could mean there.
click "fewer"
wait 500
shot p3-folded-again

# The letter's own line is the header it arrived with: everyone it went to,
# me included, and the copy behind a `cc:`. The conversation is *with* four
# people; this letter went to five. It came open with the panel, because it
# had not been read.
click "mail to"
key cmd+a
copy "me@prepor.dev, Max Ivanov <max@ivanov.dev>, Vera Kovac <vera@kovac.io>, Ana Marić <ana@maric.hr> · cc: Tom Weber <tom@weber.de>"
shot p4-letter

# Answering everyone is its own link, and it is on the bar because this
# letter named somebody besides Elena and me. The sheet comes up addressed
# to all of them at once.
click "reply all"
wait 900
shot p5-reply-all
click "to"
key cmd+a
copy "elena.p@gmail.com, max@ivanov.dev, vera@kovac.io, ana@maric.hr, tom@weber.de"
quit
