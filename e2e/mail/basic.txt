# args:
# env:
#
# The join and replace journey, on mail. A store nobody has booted comes up
# on the inbox — the first root the app list offers — so this is also what a
# person sees first. Assertions are the clicks: a label that does not resolve
# fails the run, so "the reader opened on that mail" is checked by the run
# rather than by a human reading a screenshot.

wait 900
shot m1-inbox

# -- a row previews its conversation, joined ---------------------------------

# A press on a row moves the cursor and previews what it opens: the reader
# lands joined to the right and the list keeps focus. The conversation is
# marked read by the open, so its row loses its bold.
click "Q3 infra"
wait 800
shot m2-joined

# The unread letter of it opened with the panel, and is addressed by its
# sender and date — which resolves only if the reader really opened on it.
click "Vera Kovac · aug 31 09:14"
wait 500

# -- the next row replaces the joined panel ----------------------------------

click "Sat hike"
wait 800
shot m3-replaced
click "Elena Petrova · aug 30 18:20"
wait 500

# -- reply, type, send -------------------------------------------------------

# The reader's bar carries the two links that answer a conversation. Reply
# opens a sheet joined to it, and the sheet starts in its body: what is typed
# next lands in the letter and nowhere else.
click "reply"
wait 900
type "Early start suits me — 7:30 at the trailhead."
wait 500
shot m4-compose

# Tab walks the ring, wrapping from the body round to the recipient. A
# one-line field takes its text selected, as a form's does, so what is typed
# replaces it; the body keeps its caret, because a letter is not a value to
# type over.
key tab
wait 400
type "elena.p@gmail.com"
wait 400
key tab
wait 400
type "Saturday"
wait 500
shot m5-fields

# Send is a side effect: a toast, and the sheet closes behind it.
click "send"
wait 700
shot m6-sent

# -- un-joining, and closing -------------------------------------------------

# Moving the joined reader away un-joins it: the bridge goes, and it is a
# panel of its own in the column to the left.
key cmd+shift+left
wait 900
shot m7-unjoined

key cmd+w
wait 800
shot m8-closed

# The list is alive afterwards: a row that was never touched still opens.
click "that airport book"
wait 800
shot m9-inbox-after
quit
