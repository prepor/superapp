# An answer that ran out of room, and the verb that asks for the rest. The
# fake stops a message with *cut* in it on `length`, which the transcript
# marks *cut short* in the muted line under the turn — and that mark is what
# puts **continue** on the bar.
#
# *continue* is a send like any other: it files *Continue.* as the person's
# own turn and the round that answers it is a round. So the assertion is the
# click on what came back, and on the bar's letting go of the verb once the
# answer is whole.

wait 900

key cmd 2
wait 500
type "new chat"
wait 500
key enter
wait 900

type "give me the cut version"
wait 500
key enter
wait 900
click "This answer is long and it"
wait 400
click "cut short"
wait 400
shot k1-cut-short

# The bar wears it now, and pressing it asks.
click "continue"
wait 900
click "… and here is the rest."
wait 400

# The person's own turn is in the transcript, in their own words.
click "Continue."
wait 400
shot k2-continued
quit
