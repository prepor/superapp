# env:
#
# A panel as context. `cmd+shift+a` on the inbox opens a chat joined to it
# with the inbox's chip in the composer; the chip is a reference, so a press
# on it goes to the panel it points at. What is sent renders the chip for the
# model, which is what the fake reads back — *You are looking at inbox.*
# resolves only if the panel's own context reached the request.
#
# In the chat, the same chord adds the chip of the panel the chat is joined
# from — the second half of the rule — and its `×` takes it off again. A chip
# that has gone with a turn wears no `×`: there is no unasking it.

wait 900

key cmd+shift+a
wait 900
shot c1-chip
click "chip inbox"
wait 500

# The caret goes back to the composer by its own name.
click "ask"
wait 500
type "what am I looking at"
wait 500
key enter
wait 900
click "You are looking at inbox."
wait 400

# The chip went with the turn: it stands over the words that carried it, and
# the composer is empty of chips.
click "chip inbox"
wait 400
shot c2-sent

# The chord again, from inside the chat: the panel it is joined from. The
# press above left the keyboard on the inbox, so the chat takes it back
# first — the chord is about the focused panel.
click "ask"
wait 500
key cmd+shift+a
wait 700
click "× inbox"
wait 500
shot c3-dropped
quit
