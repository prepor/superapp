# args: --grid 4x3 --window 380x780
# env:
#
# The chat on the cover display: a 380×780 window on a 4×3 grid, which is what
# the outer screen of a folding phone gets. The chat asks for six by six and
# gets the whole board, so the transcript is the screen and the composer sits
# at its foot; when the soft keyboard shows, the shell shortens the workspace
# by the occlusion and the panel makes room by being shorter — the composer
# stays where it is, at the foot of whatever room is left.
#
# `cmd+shift+a` has no glass equivalent — a long press on a header is already
# *pick the panel up* — so the chat's own **add panel** is the phone's way
# into the context a chord opens on the desktop: a field over the panels that
# are open, one pick apiece.

wait 900
shot f1-inbox

key cmd 2
wait 500
type "new chat"
wait 500
key enter
wait 900
shot f2-chat

# The verb stands the field up and hands it the keyboard; what is typed is
# matched against the open panels' titles, and enter takes what the offer is
# showing.
click "add panel"
wait 600
type "inb"
wait 700
shot f3-picking
key enter
wait 700

# The pick is the panel's own chip, in the composer, and it goes with what is
# sent — so the answer names the panel the chip carried.
click "chip inbox"
wait 500
click "ask"
wait 400
type "what am I looking at"
wait 500
key enter
wait 900
click "You are looking at inbox."
wait 400
shot f4-answered
quit
