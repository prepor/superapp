# args:
# env:
#
# The sweep: the gestures and chords the other shell suites do not cover, in
# one walk. A fresh un-joined panel from a modified click, an in-panel scroll
# that keeps the pinned chrome still, the filter's own keys, and `esc` handing
# the keyboard back from a live field.

wait 900

# -- cmd+click is always a fresh, un-joined panel -----------------------------

# A plain press previews, joined. A modified one opens beside it instead, on
# no chain at all — so both readers are on screen and each answers for itself.
click "Q3 infra"
wait 900
cmdclick "Sat hike"
wait 900
shot p1-fresh-panel
click "Elena Petrova · aug 30 18:20"
wait 700

# -- an in-panel scroll -------------------------------------------------------

# Back on the list: its filter is the one control neither reader has, and a
# press in it takes the panel's focus with it. From there the cursor walks
# down and drags the view; the filter and the header are pinned and stay put.
click "filter"
wait 700
key down 40
wait 900
shot p2-scrolled
key up 40
wait 900
click "Q3 infra"
wait 900
shot p3-back-at-the-top

# -- the filter's own keys ----------------------------------------------------

# `/` puts the caret in the filter; typing narrows; `enter` takes the first row
# and hands the keyboard back to the list; `enter` again opens it un-joined.
key /
wait 600
type "vera"
wait 900
shot p4-filtered
key enter
wait 600
key enter
wait 900
shot p5-opened-from-the-filter
click "Vera Kovac · aug 31 09:14"
wait 700

# -- esc leaves a live field --------------------------------------------------

# The reader's `reply` opens a sheet that starts in its body. `esc` hands the
# keyboard back, and the sheet's own verb then closes it.
key cmd+r
wait 900
key esc
wait 600
click "discard"
wait 900
shot p6-discarded
quit
