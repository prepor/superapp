# The row is the conversation, the panel is the whole of it.
#
# Assertions are the clicks: a closed message is addressed by its sender and
# the line it previews, an open one by its sender and date — so "this message
# opened in place" is checked by the run rather than by a human reading a
# screenshot.

wait 900

# Max's conversation folds three letters into one row: my note (in Sent),
# his reply, and his second reply with a quoted tail. Opening it previews
# the whole thread — the older two closed, one line each, the newest open.
click "superapp panel model"
wait 900
shot h1-thread

# A closed row opens in place when pressed. It is addressed by its sender
# and the first line its author wrote, which is what it previews.
click "Andrey Rudenko: Wrote up the panel model"
wait 700
shot h2-opened

# The same row, open, is addressed by sender and date; pressing it folds the
# letter back and the panel asks for fewer rows again.
click "Andrey Rudenko · aug 29 14:02"
wait 700
shot h3-closed

# The newest letter came open with the panel, and the quote it was written
# over is folded behind one line. A press unfolds it in place.
click "› quoted"
wait 700
shot h4-quote-unfolded

# The letter itself is a selectable run: a drag across it is a real
# selection, which is what a passage one wants to quote back needs.
selectall "mail body"
wait 500
shot h5-selected

# The whole conversation files as one: both of Max's inbox letters go, the
# row goes with them, and undo brings all of it back.
click "archive"
wait 900
shot h6-archived
key cmd+z
wait 900
key cmd+z
wait 900
click "superapp panel model"
wait 800
shot h7-undone
quit
