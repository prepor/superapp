# args:
# env:
#
# Walk, read, file, next — and the batch that does the same to a marked set.
#
# Assertions are the clicks: "the preview opened on the right mail", "that
# row is gone", "the cursor carried on" and "undo put it back" are all
# checked by a label resolving, not by a human reading a screenshot.

wait 900

# -- the cursor walk previews -------------------------------------------------

# A press on a row moves the cursor and previews what it opens, joined —
# without taking focus, so the keys keep walking the list rather than
# scrolling a letter.
click "Q3 infra"
wait 800
shot t1-preview

# Still the list's keyboard: one press down walks to the next conversation
# and the preview follows it. If focus had escaped into the reader, this
# arrow would have scrolled a letter and the mail below would never resolve.
key down
wait 800
shot t2-walked
click "GitHub · aug 31 08:02"
wait 500

# -- filing from the reader's own bar -----------------------------------------

# Archive is the reader's button. It files the conversation, closes every
# panel reading it, and moves the list that was driving it on to the next
# row — one action, so one undo takes all three back.
click "archive"
wait 900
shot t3-archived

# The cursor stood still and the row under it left, so it is now on the one
# below — previewed, which is what this resolving proves.
click "Max Ivanov · aug 31 07:30"
wait 500
shot t4-carried-on

# Two steps back, because the walk that followed the filing is a preview
# like any other and so a node of its own: the first undo closes what the
# cursor moved onto, the second gives the conversation back — to the inbox,
# to the panel that was reading it, and to the row the list had.
key cmd+z
wait 900
shot t5-walk-undone
key cmd+z
wait 900
shot t6-undone
click "GitHub · aug 31 08:02"
wait 600

# -- the batch ----------------------------------------------------------------

# Space marks the row under the cursor and the bar grows the verbs over the
# marked set, with their count; shift+down marks the row it leaves and the
# row it lands on.
click "Q3 infra"
wait 700
type " "
wait 400
key shift+down
wait 700
shot t7-marked

# Two conversations filed in one action: the marks come off with them, so
# the count goes off the bar. It closes nothing — the cursor stands where it
# stood, which is now the row that took its place, and previews it.
click "archive 2"
wait 900
shot t8-batch
click "Elena Petrova · aug 30 18:20"
wait 500

# Two steps back, for the reason the reader's *archive* needs two: the walk
# that followed the batch is a preview like any other. The second undo brings
# the mails back *and* the marks that filed them — the batch verb resolving
# again is the proof, and the bar's own `clear` empties the set.
key cmd+z
wait 900
key cmd+z
wait 900
shot t9-marks-restored
click "clear"
wait 600
shot t10-cleared

# The list is alive afterwards, with nothing marked and no bar in the way.
click "that airport book"
wait 800
quit
